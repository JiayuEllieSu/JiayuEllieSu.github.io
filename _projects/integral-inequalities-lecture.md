**Presented:** June 2026  
**Presenter:** Ellie Su  
**Setting:** Math Circle  
**Materials:** Seven-page lecture handout

In June 2026, I gave a Math Circle lecture on **integral inequalities**, focusing on techniques for proving sharp bounds and identifying equality cases. We began with nonnegativity and completing the square, then developed the integral Cauchy–Schwarz inequality and its weighted form.

The lecture also introduced **Jensen's inequality** through convexity and **Chebyshev's integral inequality** for similarly ordered functions. The handout includes worked examples and ten practice problems ranging from foundational applications to olympiad-level challenges.
