**Research project:** 2024  
**Presentation:** Poster, Joint Mathematics Meetings (JMM), 2025

This project explored how wavelet-based analysis and tensor regression could be used to study the genetic architecture of Alzheimer's disease. Wavelets provide a way to examine patterns at multiple scales, while tensor regression offers a mathematical framework for modeling structured, high-dimensional data. The project investigated how these approaches could be brought together in the analysis of complex genetic information.

**Presentation:** Presented as a poster at the 2025 Joint Mathematics Meetings.
