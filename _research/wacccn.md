**Lead researcher and first author** | Independent research collaboration, 2025

WACCCN is a computational framework for inferring cell-cell communication and identifying tissue niches from spatial transcriptomics and histology. It integrates multiscale wavelet-derived orientation features into anisotropic signaling models, alongside morphology-aware gating and hierarchical niche segmentation. The project explores how incorporating tissue architecture can refine spatial communication estimates.

I led the framework's development, including the anisotropic kernels, computational analysis, figures, and manuscript preparation, in collaboration with my coauthors. The study examines oral squamous cell carcinoma and breast cancer spatial transcriptomics datasets.
