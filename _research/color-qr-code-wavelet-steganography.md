**Research project:** 2023  
**Presentation:** Poster, Joint Mathematics Meetings (JMM), 2024

This collaborative project explored ways to increase the amount of information that can be stored in color QR codes. It examined the use of color-based encoding together with wavelet-domain steganography, in which additional information can be embedded into image representations at different scales. Denoising algorithms were considered as a way to address image distortion that can arise during encoding and recovery.

The project brought together ideas from wavelet analysis, image processing, and information encoding.

**Presentation:** Presented as a poster at the 2024 Joint Mathematics Meetings.
