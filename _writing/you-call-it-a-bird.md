<div class="poem" style="white-space: pre-line;">
You think it's a bird, something
that flies. You are wrong.
It is a rat. It knows
the dark tunnels you pretend
aren't there. It survives
on what you drop, on neglect,
on the crumbs of a look
between strangers on the Q train.
It has sharp teeth
for chewing through the pretty lies.
It does not sing; it scratches
in the walls of your sleep.
Its tail is a quick, ugly truth
you almost saw, then didn't.

Don't try to dress it in feathers.
Don't ask it to carry a message
over the river. It is not
a noble thing. It is the thing
that endures the flood,
that breeds in the wreck,
that looks you in the eye
from the tracks
and does not blink.

Its beauty is not in its flight,
but in its stubborn, grimy life.
In the fact that it remains
long after the trains have passed,
still hungry, still unafraid.

And if you listen,
past the last train,
past the dark,
you will know it by its small,
persistent breathing.

It was your dream.
It never left.
</div>
