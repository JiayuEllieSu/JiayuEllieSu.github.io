<div class="poem" style="white-space: pre-line;">
When clouds give birth to rain,
the raindrop is born pristine—
a fleeting pearl of sky.
But as it falls toward the earth,
it gathers dust and grime,
layer by layer,
until it hits the dirt and dies.
Yet in that dying,
there is a gift:
it births the flora and fauna,
seeps deep into the fertile dark,
and finds at last its fullest shape—
not high, but deep;
not pure, but true.
Then sun ascends the waiting sky
and lifts each drop with gentle warmth,
not to erase,
but to remember:
the one becomes the many,
and a thousand pristine lives
rise again.
</div>
