*Death and the Desire to Continue*

**Revised manuscript, November 2026 · 84 pages**

[Read the revised manuscript (PDF)]({{ page.manuscript_pdf | relative_url }})

I began *Blue to Blue* with a question that I could not put aside: when I imagine my own death, why do I keep imagining someone there to experience the absence? I wanted to understand what, exactly, I was afraid of losing, and what it would mean for me to survive.

The book begins with burial and the recognition of death, then turns to memory, consciousness, and personal identity. It considers religious and philosophical accounts of what might follow death, including resurrection, rebirth, materialism, and the possibility that there is no continuation. The final part returns to the living: attention, love, work, suffering, and the choices we make without a final answer about what happens to us.

The manuscript has five parts, 28 chapters, and 18 poems woven into the prose. The poems do not always agree with the essays. I wanted them to have room to express fears, doubts, and convictions that a philosophical argument might leave unresolved.

This is a revised draft, not a finished book. I expect to keep changing both the prose and the poems.
