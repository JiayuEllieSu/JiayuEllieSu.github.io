<div class="poem" style="white-space: pre-line;">
Rain washes away memories,
soft as a thumb erasing chalk.
Streets slick with the ghosts of footsteps,
gutters humming a tune I almost forgot.

Each puddle a small, trembling mirror—
I look in and see someone else.
The clouds have no use for names,
just keep drifting, empty as bells.

By dusk, the scent of wet iron
and cut grass. A child’s torn shoe
on the curb. Nothing grieves here—
only water returning to water, blue to blue.
</div>
